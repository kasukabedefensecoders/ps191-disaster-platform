How the queue is ordered
Never-verified zones come first, then zones the 72-hour forecast has pushed toward elevated risk, then scheduled re-verification past the 90-day currency threshold. The officer does not choose what to survey — the platform's own data gaps do.
Offline-first mechanics
Every capture writes to IndexedDB before any network attempt, so a submission survives a dead link, a closed tab, and a dead battery.
Each submission carries a client-generated UUID. The server upserts on that key, so a retried sync after a partial failure cannot duplicate the record.
Payload is 4 KB of fields plus one compressed photo — sized for an 18 kbps VSAT link, not for broadband.
Conflicts are last-write-wins on the submission itself. The unreviewed tag, not a merge algorithm, is the real safeguard — a supervisor resolves disputed data.
Try it
Flip the network toggle to offline, complete a survey, and it lands in the device queue instead of the server. Sync it from the zone list to clear the queue.
